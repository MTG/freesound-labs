Generative diffusion models have emerged as promising solutions for speech enhancement (SE), particularly in unlabeled and unseen noisy scenarios. However, their real-world deployment faces three fundamental challenges: limited generalization to complex dynamic noise conditions beyond training distributions, excessive computational demands from iterative sampling that hinder real-time implementation, and dependence on large-scale paired data for optimal performance. To address these challenges, we propose G-DiffuMSE - a novel graph-guided diffusion framework that integrates multidimensional spatial-temporal graph convolutional networks (STGCN) with conditional diffusion processes. Leveraging its inherent ability to capture spatiotemporal correlations in multichannel signals, STGCN reconstructs the target structural information, guiding the diffusion sampling process for more efficient denoising. Additionally, attention-based noise-conditional scenery modeling, applied through scene graphs, enhances robustness against un-seen or dynamic noise conditions, mitigating the generalization limitations of conventional diffusion models. Our framework demonstrates superior performance and strong robustness across various challenging scenarios, effectively handling both seen and unseen noise environments.
