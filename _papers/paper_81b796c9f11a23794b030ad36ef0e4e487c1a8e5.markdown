Recent advancements in voice authentication systems have raised growing concerns about their vulnerability to spoofing attacks. While multi-channel speech processing provides spatial cues that can significantly enhance anti-spoofing robustness, research in this direction remains scarce due to the high cost of data collection and the lack of suitable pre-trained models. This study addresses these challenges by constructing a synthetic four-channel noisy speech dataset based on the MultiSV data generation framework, using clean speech from ASVspoof2019 and environmental noise from FSD50K. To leverage powerful single-channel self-supervised models for multi-channel processing, we propose a transfer learning framework that fine-tunes the WavLM model with Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA). This design effectively enables the model to exploit spatial information and handle multichannel speech inputs. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed system substantially outperforms the ASVspoof 5 baseline, achieving a significant reduction in Equal Error Rate (EER). Beyond empirical gains, this work provides a scalable strategy for adapting large pre-trained SSL models to multichannel speech applications. The proposed framework and dataset offer valuable insights for future research on spatial-aware speech processing.
