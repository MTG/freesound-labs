Audio-only detection of small unmanned aerial vehicles is essential when vision degrades at long range, under occlusion, or at night. This paper benchmarks frozen pretrained audio embeddings paired with simple classifiers. The study covers CLAP, PaSST, BEATs, HTS-AT, and the lightweight YAMNet. A 52-class dataset of 2,963 five-second clips combines ESC-50 negatives, curated UAV recordings from DroneAudio and Creative Commons videos, a speech class from Common Voice, and our field recordings. Evaluation reports accuracy, macro-F1, and end-to-end throughput.CLAP with a linear SVM delivers the best performance with accuracy 0.988 and macro-F1 0.987. PaSST with logistic regression is a close second with macro-F1 0.981 while running at 83.8 tasks per second compared with 46.4 for CLAP with a linear SVM. YAMNet with logistic regression is the fastest at 188.0 tasks per second but yields a macro-F1 of 0.857. Linear heads consistently outperform the tree model and capture most of the discriminative power of the embeddings. Recommended operating points are CLAP with a linear SVM for accuracy-first analysis and PaSST with logistic regression for real-time deployment, with integration via late fusion into a visual detector.
