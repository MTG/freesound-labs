Due to the different durations of different sound events, models with fixed receptive fields are less suitable for sound event detection. Therefore, it is necessary to devise an efficient method to extract multi-scale features. We implement the SED system based on a multi-scale feature extraction module. This method can extract multi-scale features, overcome the insufficiency of fixed receptive field, and help the system identify different sound events. We further discuss the effect of different convolution scales and the performance of specific sound events, and experimentally show that increasing the receptive field of large scales can improve the performance of longer duration events. This may be because the large-scale receptive field can more effectively extract the overall information of the sound. Due to the high acquisition cost of strongly labeled data, we implement the Mean Teacher (MT) method for training which is a semi-supervised learning technique. The segment-based F1 score of the method outperforms the baseline system by 12.1% in experiments conducted on the DCASE 2019 task4 dataset.
