Audio is one of the most important elements of creative media productions. However, interfaces for searching, creating and editing auditory data are often determined by the underlying technical parameters. These parameters usually describe neither their meaning for the sound nor the qualities that are expressed in the sound. As a result, such interfaces rarely offer an intuitive and expressive interaction. A translation process from the artistic idea to the technical parameters must take place first. Therefore, the mental model of audio is analyzed here with an associative elicitation through sketches as abstract visual representations. For this purpose, sketches representing sounds are assessed and analyzed with an online study platform. So far, a total of 3250 sketches for 173 audio objects were created by 164 participants within 4 study sessions. Furthermore, a workflow for deriving the classification of the audio-visual associations is proposed with a discussion for a first derived classification from the resulting sketch associations. This classification is a possible starting point for the development of tools and conveys an awareness of the expressive possibilities in the use of visual associations through to affordances e.g. for audio-visual design, or for recommendation systems.
